No app to install. No dashboard to learn. No new account to create. The entire product is a WhatsApp conversation, because that is where you already are.

How it works

  1. You message Grovio first

    Always. Grovio never starts a conversation, never sends promotions, and never messages you out of the blue.

  2. It connects to your own delivery account, once

    You authorise it through the delivery platform's own secure sign-in page. Grovio never sees your password.

  3. It suggests real options, and explains why

    Every restaurant, price, rating and delivery time comes from a live response from the delivery platform. Nothing is invented or estimated.

  4. You confirm, and it orders

    Grovio only ever orders something it has just shown you, and only after you pick it. Then it confirms the total and the ETA.

What it remembers

Your preferences

Budget, favourite cuisines, the area you order to, dietary rules.

Allergies and dislikes

Anything you must avoid is filtered out of every recommendation.

What you've ordered

So it can stop asking questions you've already answered.

What Grovio will never do

  • Never order without your say-so. Every order needs you to choose an option that was just shown to you.
  • Never invent a restaurant, price or delivery time. If a detail didn't come back from the delivery platform, Grovio doesn't say it.
  • Never ask for a password, OTP or card details. Sign-in happens on the delivery platform's own page, not in the chat.
  • Never message you first, and never send marketing.
  • Never sell your data. Not to anyone, for any purpose.

Who it's for

People who spend more time choosing dinner than eating it. Grovio is a personal assistant for individuals ordering their own food — it is not a restaurant tool, a point-of-sale system, or a business platform.
