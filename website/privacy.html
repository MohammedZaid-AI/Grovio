Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

Grovio is a personal food-ordering assistant that runs inside WhatsApp. This policy explains exactly what it stores, why, who else sees it, and how to make it go away. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Grovio stores your WhatsApp number, your conversation with it, your food preferences, and your order history — so it can do its job without asking you the same questions forever. It never sells your data, never shares it for advertising, and never sees your delivery-platform password. Email hello@grovio.app and everything is deleted.

1. Who we are

Grovio is operated by [LEGAL NAME], [FULL ADDRESS], India. For anything in this policy — including deletion requests — contact hello@grovio.app.

2. What we collect

Only what the service needs to work. There is no tracking, no advertising identifier, and no data broker anywhere in this list.

WhatWhy
Your WhatsApp phone number It is how the service identifies you. There is no separate account, username or password.
The messages you send and the replies you receive To hold a coherent conversation, and to understand a follow-up like "the second one".
Food preferences you tell us — budget, cuisines, allergies, dietary rules, delivery area So recommendations fit you and you are not asked the same thing twice.

We do not collect or store: your delivery-platform password, card or UPI details, your precise location, your contacts, your photos, or anything from any other chat on your phone. Grovio can only see messages you send directly to it.

3. Artificial intelligence

Grovio uses a large language model to understand what you write and to phrase its replies. The content of your messages, along with your stored preferences and recent conversation, is sent to a third-party AI provider (currently Groq and/or OpenAI-compatible services) for that purpose.

Those providers process the text to generate a response. We do not permit your data to be used to train their models. Please avoid sending Grovio anything sensitive you would not want processed this way — it only needs to know about food.

4. Connecting a delivery account

To place an order, you connect your own account with a delivery platform such as Swiggy. This uses the platform's own secure sign-in page (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE).

  • You type your credentials on the platform's page, never in the chat and never to us. We never see them.
  • We receive only an access token, which is stored encrypted at rest and used solely to search and place orders you have explicitly confirmed.
  • Grovio will never ask you for a password, an OTP, or card details. If anything claiming to be Grovio does, it is not us.
  • You can disconnect at any time by saying so in the chat. The token is deleted immediately.

5. Who your data is shared with

Only the parties needed to deliver the service. Never anyone else, and never for money.

WhoWhat they get
Meta / WhatsApp Your messages, as the carrier of the conversation. Governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
Delivery platforms (e.g. Swiggy) The searches and orders you ask for, made against your own account there.
AI providers (e.g. Groq, OpenAI-compatible) The message text needed to generate a reply. See section 3.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising or marketing. We do not build profiles for anyone else. We may disclose data if required by law, and would tell you unless legally prevented.

6. How long it is kept

Preferences and order history are kept while you use Grovio, because that is the feature — an assistant that forgets you every week is not useful. Delete them at any time by asking.

Delivery-platform tokens expire on the platform's own schedule and are deleted as soon as you disconnect. Conversation state — what you were in the middle of — expires automatically within an hour.

7. Your rights

Email hello@grovio.app from the WhatsApp number you use with Grovio, and you can:

  • Get a copy of everything stored about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong — or just tell Grovio in the chat, which is faster
  • Delete everything — preferences, history, orders, tokens
  • Withdraw consent by disconnecting your delivery account or by asking us to stop

Requests are handled by a person, within 30 days and usually much sooner. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

8. Security

  • Delivery-platform tokens are encrypted at rest. If the encryption key is missing, the service refuses to store a token at all rather than fall back to plain text.
  • Every incoming WhatsApp request is cryptographically verified before it is acted on, so nobody can impersonate you or WhatsApp.
  • Only explicitly authorised phone numbers can place orders.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you.

9. Children

Grovio is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will delete the data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and we will tell you in the chat before the change takes effect.
